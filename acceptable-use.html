Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

You may use NoContext only in a lawful and respectful way that does not damage the service or other people.

Prohibited conduct

  • Unauthorized access to accounts, APIs, servers, databases or security controls.
  • Using the service to distribute malware, malicious code, phishing pages or credential-stealing tools.
  • Abusing rate limits, automated endpoints or infrastructure in a way that degrades service for others.
  • Impersonating NoContext, its staff, another user or another organization.
  • Using stolen, shared, fraudulent or unauthorized license keys or API credentials.
  • Using NoContext to facilitate unlawful activity or to violate another person's intellectual-property, privacy or other rights.
  • Attempting to bypass account bans, access controls, security systems or product restrictions.

Security reporting

Please report suspected security vulnerabilities through the official Contact page. Do not exploit a vulnerability beyond what is reasonably necessary to demonstrate the issue, and do not access or retain other users' information.

Enforcement

We may rate-limit, suspend, revoke credentials, disable licenses or terminate accounts when reasonably necessary to protect the service, users or legal rights. We may also preserve information and cooperate with lawful requests when required.

Nothing in this policy authorizes conduct that applicable law prohibits, and nothing here waives rights that cannot legally be waived.
