Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NoContext handles information when you use the NoContext website, account system, software-related services, browser experiences and developer API.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use NoContext, we may collect information you provide directly, including a username, email address, account password information in protected hashed form, support requests and other information you choose to send us.

We may also receive technical and security information associated with requests to our services, such as IP address, browser or device information, timestamps, authentication/session identifiers, security events, and API usage information. License and key services may also record license status, product, creation or activation dates, expiration dates and limited usage timestamps.

When you use Discord authentication or connect Discord to an account, we may receive identifiers and basic profile information that Discord provides through the permissions you approve, such as your Discord user ID, username and email address when available.

2. How we use information

  • Provide, authenticate and secure accounts and sessions.
  • Provide licenses, keys, developer API access and requested services.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access and attacks.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate about the service.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, measure and improve the website and services.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

NoContext uses essential cookies or similar browser storage for functions such as maintaining signed-in sessions and security state. These technologies are necessary for requested account features.

4. Service providers

We use third-party infrastructure and service providers to operate NoContext. Depending on the feature used, this can include hosting, database, authentication, communications, Discord integration, key-verification services and code delivery platforms. These providers may process information on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, comply with legal requirements, or otherwise satisfy a legitimate business need. Security and audit records may be retained for longer where reasonably necessary.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information we process. Passwords are hashed rather than stored as plaintext. Authentication tokens are handled as protected credentials. No method of transmission or storage is guaranteed to be completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

NoContext is not intended to collect personal information from children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from a child under 13 without a legally required parental-consent process. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13 in a manner that requires consent and did not receive the required consent, we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

Users must meet the minimum age required to use the service under applicable law. Users who are minors should use the service only with the permission of a parent or legal guardian where required.

8. U.S. state privacy rights

Depending on where you live and whether the applicable thresholds are met, state privacy laws may give you rights such as access, deletion, correction, portability, or opting out of certain sales or sharing of personal information. We will process verified requests as required by applicable law.

California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including rights to know, delete, correct, opt out of sale or sharing where applicable, limit certain uses of sensitive personal information where applicable, and receive non-discriminatory treatment.

9. How to make a privacy request

For privacy requests, contact us through the official NoContext contact channel listed on our Contact page. Tell us what request you are making and enough information for us to reasonably locate the relevant account or data. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services or legal obligations change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

This policy is intended to describe the current NoContext website practices. U.S. privacy requirements depend on the information collected, the services offered, and applicable state and federal thresholds. Have this policy reviewed by a qualified U.S. attorney before relying on it as a complete compliance program.
