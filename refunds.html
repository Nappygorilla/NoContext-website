Refund Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This policy explains how NoContext handles refund requests for digital products, licenses and other paid services.

1. Digital products

Because digital licenses and downloadable products can be accessed or delivered electronically, purchases are generally final after delivery, except where a refund is required by applicable law or expressly offered on the product's purchase page.

2. Defective or unavailable products

Contact support when a purchased product cannot be delivered, a license is materially defective, or an advertised paid feature was not provided. We may troubleshoot, replace the license, extend access or issue a refund when appropriate.

3. Duplicate or unauthorized charges

Report suspected duplicate charges or unauthorized transactions promptly. We may request transaction details or other information needed to investigate the issue. Do not send full payment-card numbers or security codes through support channels.

4. Refund process

Start a request through the official Contact page. Include the account email, transaction or order identifier, product involved, purchase date and a clear explanation of the issue. We will not ask you to post private credentials publicly.

5. Third-party payment providers

If a payment is processed by a third-party provider, that provider may also have its own refund, chargeback and dispute rules. Nothing here limits rights that cannot legally be waived.

Refund eligibility can depend on the product, how the transaction was processed, where the customer lives and applicable consumer-protection laws. This policy does not waive non-waivable rights.
